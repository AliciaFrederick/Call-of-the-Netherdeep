## Secrets and Clues

- [ ] The leader of Ank'Harel is a dragon who works with the Cobalt Soul to locate and obtain rare magical artifacts for their horde, under the pretense of protecting them and keeping them out of the wrong hands. The horde contains a Luxon beacon. 
- [ ] Perjeon's treasure map leads to a dragon horde (the leader of AnkHarel's), and his former shipmate wants the map for himself. 
- [ ] Perjeon wants to buy an airship and needs a connection to the builders in Ank'Harel, along with 100,000 gp. Once he knows his map leads to a horde, he'll be tempted to follow it. 
- [ ] Aza wants to steal a beacon of the Luxon. She will find one if she joins Perjeon to search for the horde.
- [ ] Thrast will speak to the dragon and provoke its wrath when it lears a beacon has been stolen
