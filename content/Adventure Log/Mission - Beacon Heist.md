## Background

The Veil is no mere guild of pickpockets and ruffians. The elite thieves of this multinational organization pull off impossible heists, targeting crown jewels, national secrets, and other heavily guarded prizes. A decade ago, the elf [Nixylanna Vidorant](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#nixylanna%20vidorant_kftgv) joined the Veil, where she partnered with a human named Ilena Hapayhari. These friendly rivals made a good pair. Vidorant was skilled with picking locks and deftly getting into tough places, and Ilena used her connections and quick thinking to research targets and talk the pair's way out of trouble.

As the years passed, the duo rose in the ranks of the Veil. But their partnership abruptly ended when they stole the Silvered Dagger, an heirloom once used to assassinate the Pirate King. Ilena's parents were brutally murdered at the hands of a pirate wielding that dagger, and she desperately wants to claim it for herself. The heist seemed like any other—until thirty guards suddenly surrounded the pair of thieves. Vidorant nimbly escaped via the roof with the dagger in hand, but Ilena was caught. She avoided imprisonment by calling in favors with powerful friends, but when she tracked down Vidorant, she refused to hand over the dagger despite her claim to it.

Ilena claimed Vidorant set her up and alerted the authorities. Meanwhile, Vidorant claimed innocence, insisting Ilena was covering for her own inability to escape the situation. Whatever the truth, she kept the dagger, the once-friendly rivalry turned bitter, and the two have tried to outmaneuver each other ever since.

Recently, their uneasy relationship changed again with the death of the longtime leader of the Veil. Vidorant and Ilena each competed to win leadership of the society, calling in favors from every ally they had in the organization. Unfortunately for Vidorant, Ilena proved defter at internal politics, winning leadership of the society—and getting Vidorant expelled in the process. Ilena promised harsh retaliation against anyone who helped Vidorant, ensuring none of her former allies would stand against her.

Weeks after her takeover of the Veil, Ilena made an even bigger move against Vidorant. She promised a favor to any existing or aspiring member of the Veil who steals the Silvered Dagger and delivers it to her. This challenge is the talk of scoundrel circles as everyone vies to steal the dagger from Vidorant. Doing so won't be easy, as Vidorant keeps her treasures in her secure vault on the outskirts of the city.
