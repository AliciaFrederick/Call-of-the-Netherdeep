Names and descriptions:

- Mom: Soliana Beachgift. The guild master for the jewelers of the port of Zoon. She was raised in traditional fashion with the intention of her becoming the next guild master as her father before her. Tall, blonde, care free, serious about the guild, french accent. Think wine mom.    
- Dad: Barclay “the devil” Jones, now goes by Micheal Beachgift. From port Damali. Grew up an orphan on the streets. Got on a ship’s crew as quickly as he could. After a few years of sailing, his ship was attacked by pirates. He accepted their offer to join their crew rather than die. Worked his way up the ranks and eventually became captain. Went on to terrorize the seas robbing merchant vessels. Midsized, burly, gruff. Think stereotypical pirate.    
- Any ship friends: A group of younger crew that Perjeon would go carousing with at ports. Chogs Skullcrusher: a flirty goblin girl. Nisdosh Thra’ak: a radical (socialist) half orc boy. Johnny Johnson: a mischievous human boy.    
- Your rival's name is [[Ilena]] (human woman with copper skin and curly raven hair): They were injured when Perjeon overstuffed a cannon with black powder.    

Timeline with these events at a minimum:

- Parents met: Soliana Beachcraft was traveling on vacation with her parents to port Damali. She coerced her maid (Hilda Hofmeister, halfling woman) to escape from her parents to go to a tavern for a night. There she encountered Barclay “the devil” Jones without knowing who he was. He convinced her to come to his ship where (Hilda came along) he discreetly slipped out of port before she realized what was going on. She was down for an adventure. At first he planned to ransom her back to her parents, but after he saw how excited she was for the experience he fell in love with her. He decided to return her to her parents, but she wanted to stay. She eventually fell in love with him. She started helping aboard the ship. Hilda ended up engaging in combat alongside the pirates. After a couple months Soliana became pregnant. Soliana and Barclay decided to head back to port Zoon and settle down.    
- Dad left piracy: Once back in Zoon, it was decided that Barclay would give up piracy and take on a new alias, Micheal. Since Soliana’s family was powerful they were able to craft a new history for Micheal to cover up his past and allow him to meld into polite society. Since port Zoon’s government relies on the captains Micheal could leverage his real experience to be of use.    
- Parents married: A marriage was rushed before Soliana’s pregnancy was showing    
- You were born:     
- You first spent time on a ship: Early on (12?) Micheal brought Perjeon onto one of his legitimate merchant runs. Perjeon slowly learned (not mastered) all of the jobs on the ship.    
- The last time you saw Ilena: Perjeon accidentally overpacked a cannon with black powder that Ilena was using while fending off a pirate attack. The resulting explosion hurt Ilena and she was taken to the infirmary and then eventually to a port town for more serious medical attention. Due to the chaos of the battle (and Perjeon is daddy’s special boy) no one besides Ilena knew that it was Perjeon’s fault.    
- The last time you were on a ship: A couple of months before Perjeon struck out on his own. It was a regular merchant run with his father. They were trading goods from Zoon (particularly jewelry from Soliana’s guild) up and down the Menagerie coast, as are the usual merchant runs.

#### Prophecy Rewards

Each time a character completes one of their three prophecy goals, they gain a reward of the DM’s determination. Any of the following rewards are suitable for completing a goal, or the DM might decide on unique rewards of the same general level:

- For the next 1d10 days, the character has advantage on saving throws to avoid being [frightened](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#FrightenedCondition).
- For the next 1d4 days, the character’s weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 damage of the weapon’s type.
